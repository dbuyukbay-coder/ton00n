# n00n Hackathon Demo Runbook

## What this demo proves

> n00n captures context, tests the founder’s claim, makes research boundaries visible, lets the user choose a direction, and carries that choice into a usable move and a review loop.

The fastest fixture uses **Luma**, a fictional astrology app. The Luma shortcut preloads a **sample interview transcript** so the jury can reach the research checkpoint without waiting for five separate inputs. This is a demo fixture, not the product rule. In a real project, n00n continues asking until the decision-critical coverage is sufficient.

## 3-minute judge path

### 0:00–0:20 — Start with the product

Open the app and click **Run the Luma demo**.

Say:

> “Most tools start by asking what campaign you want. n00n starts with what you are building. This shortcut preloads a sample interview so we can see the full loop.”

Luma is:

> An AI astrology app that combines birth-chart data and current transits into a short daily reflection and practical prompt.

The sample fixture contains context about:

- current behaviour and existing alternatives;
- consequence and decision friction;
- discovery and readiness;
- trust and privacy barriers;
- evidence and founder constraints.

### 0:20–0:45 — Show research, not a generic answer

Point to **Decision-critical context captured** and the research checkpoint.

Say:

> “n00n does not call the founder’s feature a strategy. It separates the founder claim, the category signal, the behaviour gap, and what is still unresolved.”

Show the source cards. Each card has:

- the official URL;
- the exact supporting quote;
- the checked date;
- the scan scope.

Say:

> “This is a focused read of three official product pages, not a universal market study. A competitor’s silence is not automatically an opportunity. It is only material for a hypothesis.”

### 0:45–1:15 — Make the choice visible

Click **Build the direction**.

Show the two options. Select the second option:

> **Build a trusted daily reflection practice.**

Say:

> “The user is not forced to accept n00n’s first recommendation. The direction becomes real only after the user chooses it.”

Point out that the selected direction changes:

- the strategic choice;
- the behaviour lens;
- the behavioural insight;
- the Not Yet boundary;
- the working plan;
- the action list.

### 1:15–1:45 — Show production, not a task list

Under the selected direction, click **Build this move** for:

> **Run a 7-Night Reflection Practice.**

The production pack contains ready-to-use materials:

- participant invitation copy;
- one-page practice copy;
- seven-night prompt sequence;
- privacy boundary copy;
- downloadable announcement visual;
- review sheet and measurement language.

Say:

> “These are not instructions to write the output later. They are editable materials generated from the selected direction. The visual is a downloadable demo asset and is clearly labelled as a sample.”

Edit one textarea in front of the jury if useful. The material is persisted to the project.

### 1:45–2:20 — Show the Desk and review loop

Open **The Daily Desk**.

Point to the selected move and current direction. Confirm the Desk does not revert to the first option.

Click **Review this move**. Enter a result such as:

> Three of ten participants returned voluntarily on nights 2–4. Two said privacy was still unclear. The ritual felt useful for sleep, but the chart explanation did not add enough value beyond journaling. Open question: can a no-data, user-written mode earn another return?

Choose **Adjust the move** or **Continue the move**.

Say:

> “The Desk does not turn one result into an automatic strategy change. It stores what happened, keeps alternative explanations visible, and lets the founder decide whether to continue, adjust, or reconsider.”

Close with:

> “n00n does not make marketing louder. It makes the next decision clearer — and carries that decision into work.”

## Optional proof of adaptive reasoning

If a judge wants to test the interview instead of using the shortcut, start a new project and answer in Turkish:

> İnsanlar yatmadan önce defterine günlük yazıyor. Önemli bir karar aramıyorlar. Defter işe yarıyor, değiştirmemek önemli bir kayıp yaratmıyor. Temel engel özel günlük bilgilerinin paylaşılması.

n00n should not ask another generic barrier question. It should surface two hypotheses:

1. privacy may be blocking trial;
2. the current notebook may already be good enough.

Then it should ask the counterfactual:

> Hiçbir kişisel bilgi paylaşmaları gerekmese, Luma’yı denemek için yeterli bir neden olur mu? Olmazsa, defterin üstüne hangi somut değeri koyması gerekir?

This is the core behavioural reasoning proof: the answer changes the hypothesis and the next question.

## What to say about the AI layer

> “The LLM is one layer inside a constrained method. It receives the idea, brand, founder claim, and conversation. It chooses the next question by decision impact and missing coverage. n00n makes competing explanations visible instead of accepting the first answer as strategy.”

Do not claim that n00n replaces a CMO, knows the market automatically, or has continuous production analytics in this MVP.

## What to say about integrations

> “Apify is the targeted research layer: official product pages, a small comparable set, and focused research questions. It enriches evidence; it does not choose the strategy.”

> “fal.ai belongs downstream. Once a move is selected, it can produce a relevant visual. It must never create the direction.”

For the current build, only describe these as connected integrations when their live result is visible in the app.

## Demo safety

- Use **Run the Luma demo** for the shortest reliable path.
- Select the second direction to prove that the downstream experience changes.
- Do not call the sample metrics live data.
- Do not call the category read a complete market study.
- Do not claim live crawling or generated creative unless the result is visible.
- If the model is slow, wait for **Listening...** to finish; do not click twice.
- If the preview shows a stale state, refresh once and start the Luma shortcut again.
