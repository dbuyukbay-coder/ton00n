# n00n MVP Brief

## What n00n is

n00n is a guided marketing-direction tool for people bringing an idea, product, or business to market.

It is **not** an “AI strategist,” a generic marketing chatbot, a campaign generator, a GTM machine, or a content calendar tool.

n00n starts with the product or idea itself, then helps the user turn that into a clearer marketing direction and a small number of measurable actions.

> **More output isn’t the answer. Better direction is.**
>
> Today’s marketing tools accelerate output. n00n builds the direction that output should come from.

The core problem is that people—especially technical founders—often fall in love with the problem they solved or the feature they built. They mistake product features for strategy:

- “We use AI.”
- “We are faster.”
- “We have a better dashboard.”
- “We need GTM.”
- “We should post more on LinkedIn.”

These are not necessarily strategic decisions. They are features, assumptions, or premature activity choices.

n00n helps the user move from **what the product does** to **what the product needs to mean in the market**, then to **what the brand should do next**.

## Who it is for

The product should not be limited to “founders” in its public definition. It is for anyone trying to bring a product, idea, new offer, or business into the market.

For the MVP demo, however, the strongest user scenario is a **technical founder building an astrology app**:

> A technical founder is building Luma, an AI astrology app that combines birth-chart data and current transits into one short daily reflection and a practical prompt for the decision someone is facing.

This is a useful demo case because the founder may begin with a feature-led request:

> “We built an AI astrology app with personalised readings. We need a launch plan.”

n00n should not accept this as a valid strategy brief. It should gradually uncover the actual problem, decision context, category reality, and most suitable first marketing move.

## What n00n must not do

n00n must not:

- Begin by asking “What do you want to change?”
- Ask the user to complete a long traditional marketing brief.
- Ask more than one question at a time.
- Present a static questionnaire.
- Produce long reports, 20-page strategy documents, or overwhelming outputs.
- Generate personas as a default output.
- Assume GTM, SEO, social media, paid advertising, content, or outbound outreach are always appropriate.
- Create generic outreach messages or mass-GTM sequences.
- Position the product as a replacement for a human marketer, CMO, or strategist.
- Become a CRM, social-media scheduler, ad-spend manager, or full marketing automation suite in the MVP.
- Treat trend tracking as the main value of marketing.
- Claim to “find missing context.” The product receives the idea and product characteristics, then builds marketing direction from there.

## The n00n Method

The first four stages are explicit in the product and should be easy for the user to understand.

### 1. Idea Understanding

n00n begins with the product or idea.

First screen:

> **What are you building?**
> Start with the idea. You don’t need a marketing plan yet.

The user can describe the product, idea, or problem in their own words. n00n should understand the basic product shape before it asks about marketing.

### 2. Adaptive Interview

The interview is the core MVP feature.

n00n asks **exactly one question at a time**. It reads the user’s answer, gives a short reflection, and selects the next most useful question. The next question is never a fixed continuation of a generic form.

The agent should internally use marketing strategy, consumer behaviour, behavioural marketing, 7P, category analysis, campaign learning, failure patterns, and different marketing-motion knowledge to decide what to ask. The user should not be overloaded with these frameworks.

The system should use them as an internal decision system, not as a visible lesson.

Example interaction:

**User:** “Our product saves time by using AI to analyse customer feedback.”

**n00n:** “I’m hearing a product capability. Before we turn it into a message, I want to understand the moment when someone needs it.”

**Next question:** “Who feels this problem most sharply today, and what are they doing instead?”

If the user says “They need to save time,” n00n should probe:

> “What does the lost time create: delay, risk, disagreement, missed opportunity, or something else?”

If the user says “We need GTM,” n00n should not produce a GTM plan. It should clarify whether a push motion, pull motion, relationship-based motion, partnership, education, workshop, product experience, thought leadership, event, PR, search, or another move is actually appropriate.

### 3. Similarity Scan

After the product is understood, n00n checks comparable products, alternatives, and category language.

It should ask questions such as:

- Are there similar products?
- How do they describe themselves?
- Is the founder’s claimed difference actually different?
- Is the difference meaningful to the buyer?
- Is the category repeating the same feature-led language?
- Is there a different use case or decision context worth owning?

A useful n00n response could be:

> “The difference you described appears in three comparable products as well. We may need to look beyond the feature and understand the decision your product changes.”

### 4. Research Plan

Research is required, but n00n should explain it simply.

Instead of showing a dense methodology, it should say:

> **Before we choose a direction, I’m checking three things.**
> 1. What your product currently claims
> 2. What comparable products claim
> 3. What decision your buyer needs to make

The scope of research may change, but research itself is never optional.

Possible research inputs:

- Product website and documentation
- Comparable products and alternatives
- Targeted search results
- Customer interviews and support conversations
- Sales notes
- Past campaigns and results
- Public customer reviews
- Relevant advertising and campaign examples
- Behavioural research when it helps explain a decision

Every insight should be clearly identified as one of:

- **Known** — confirmed fact
- **Observed** — pattern seen across evidence
- **Assumption** — plausible but unproven claim
- **Unresolved** — question that still changes the direction

Research is not the answer. It is material for a better choice.

## How strategy should work inside n00n

n00n should not present strategy as a marketing-channel plan, persona deck, or feature list.

A compact n00n **Strategy Spine** should contain only what a non-marketer needs to act:

1. **The real tension** — what is actually unresolved between the product, the market, and the buyer’s decision.
2. **The behaviour to support** — what the buyer should do differently.
3. **The direction to test** — the strategic meaning the product should pursue.
4. **Not yet** — what the company should deliberately avoid doing too early.
5. **The first move** — the smallest useful marketing action that follows from the direction.
6. **How we’ll know** — a limited, measurable review condition.

For the Luma technical-founder sample, the strategy may be:

- **The real tension:** The product starts from an AI horoscope feature, while the audience may be looking for language and confidence in a specific moment of uncertainty.
- **The behaviour to support:** Move from consuming a prediction and leaving to naming one feeling or decision and returning to it with more clarity.
- **The direction to test:** Own the five-minute reflection before a real-life decision or transition, not another daily horoscope feed.
- **Not yet:** Do not start with generic zodiac content, horoscope spam, influencer seeding, or a broad launch before one reflection ritual changes what someone does.
- **The first move:** Run a 7-Day Clarity Ritual with people who are in the middle of a real decision.
- **How we’ll know:** Measure reflection completion, decisions named, return within seven days, and whether participants report a clearer next step.

## Marketing actions must not be limited to content

After direction is established, n00n should be able to produce actions. But an action must always be linked to a strategic reason, a target behaviour, and a measurement approach.

The action library should include:

- Founder-led workshop
- Product clinic or live demo
- Event or congress participation
- Live stream
- Partnership
- PR
- Thought-leadership piece
- User research session
- Community action
- Product-led experience
- Landing page
- Sales material
- Case study
- Targeted outreach, only when appropriate
- Search / SEO, only when appropriate
- Paid media, only when appropriate
- Limited social content, only when appropriate

n00n should be able to say:

> “Do not create more social content yet. The first useful move is a small workshop because this product needs trust, explanation, and evidence of a better decision.”

## Daily use: the n00n Desk

n00n should not feel like a one-time interview that ends in a report.

It needs a daily working screen—the **n00n Desk**—that lets the user return to the direction and its real-world implications.

The Desk should show:

- **Open question:** the one unanswered question most likely to change the current direction.
- **Current direction:** a short, working statement, not a final strategy document.
- **Research plan:** what is already checked and what still needs evidence.
- **Move in motion:** the selected marketing action, its purpose, timing, and measurement.
- **Signals to notice:** campaign observations, category movements, or action outcomes that deserve attention.
- **Next review:** a finite moment when the user should reconsider the direction.

The Desk should not become a generic analytics dashboard. It is a place to keep the marketing direction alive after the initial interview.

## Apify and fal.ai

### Apify

Apify should be used as n00n’s targeted research layer, not as a generic scraping or prospecting system.

Useful MVP inputs:

- Product website crawl
- Two or three comparable-product website crawls
- Focused search questions generated by n00n

The data should help n00n compare claims, category language, proof, and decision context.

Do not use Apify for:

- mass outreach lists
- generic trend reports
- generic competitor reports
- automated lead generation
- bulk GTM messaging

### fal.ai

fal.ai should not be central to the MVP and should never create strategy.

It should only be used after a direction and a marketing move are selected. For example:

- event visual
- workshop invitation visual
- landing-page hero visual
- campaign creative direction
- live-stream announcement visual

The product should show that the visual comes **after** the strategic decision, not before it.

## MVP user experience and visual direction

The interface is English.

It should feel like a calm editorial strategy desk rather than a conventional SaaS dashboard or an AI chat interface.

Design principles:

- One thing at a time
- Clear distinction between known evidence, observations, assumptions, and unresolved questions
- Quiet, direct, confident language
- No gamification
- No loud “AI thinking” theatrics
- A small `00 → n00n` progression system
- Warm paper background, off-black type, signal lime for direction/state, subtle coral for unresolved assumptions
- `DM Mono` for labels and metadata; `Manrope` for readable questions and decisions

## MVP deliverable

The hackathon deliverable should be a **working live prototype**, supported by a short pitch—not a pitch deck alone.

A 2-minute demo can follow this sequence:

1. The founder enters a feature-led product idea.
2. n00n asks one adaptive question instead of producing a plan.
3. The founder answers; n00n reflects and asks the next most necessary question.
4. n00n shows a short similarity scan and a clear research plan.
5. n00n reveals a compact working direction.
6. n00n turns that direction into one measurable first marketing move.
7. The n00n Desk shows that this becomes an ongoing decision and review practice—not a one-off report.

## One-line product definition

> **n00n takes the product or idea you have, builds a marketing direction from it, and turns that direction into measurable next moves.**
