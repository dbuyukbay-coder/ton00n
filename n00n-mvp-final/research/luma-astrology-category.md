# Luma astrology category notes

## Demo concept

Luma is a fictional AI astrology app that combines a person’s birth chart and current transits into one short daily reflection and a practical prompt for the decision they are facing.

The founder’s starting brief is intentionally feature-led:

> We built an AI astrology app with personalised readings. We need a launch plan.

The strategic question is not whether Luma can produce another horoscope. It is whether the product can help a person turn a moment of uncertainty into a small reflection or next step.

## Official category references

### Co–Star

Source: https://www.costarastrology.com/

Co–Star describes itself as an astrology app that deciphers human relations through NASA data and “biting truth.” Its public product language includes full birth-chart readings, relationship context, and real-time insights as the planets move.

Category implication: Co–Star already occupies hyper-personalised chart interpretation, current planetary movement, and relationship context.

### The Pattern

Source: https://www.thepattern.com/

The Pattern describes itself as a personalised astrology app for understanding life patterns and relationships. Its public product language includes personality insights, relationship dynamics, timing and transits, daily guidance, and time travel through past, present, and future dates.

Category implication: The Pattern already occupies deeper self-understanding, relationship interpretation, timing, and recurring guidance.

### CHANI

Source: https://www.chani.com/app

CHANI describes its app around manifestation, self-discovery, mindfulness, healing, birth-chart readings, real-time astrology, meditation, affirmations, personalised reflection prompts, and daily guidance. It also states that its content is written and recorded by professional astrologers rather than AI.

Category implication: CHANI already occupies astrology combined with human-led reflection, wellbeing, mindfulness, and accessible education.

## Working strategic opening

The category already promises personalised readings, relationships, timing, daily guidance, self-discovery, and reflection. Luma should not enter with a generic “AI horoscope” or “more accurate daily prediction” claim before testing a distinct behaviour.

A useful first hypothesis is:

> **Luma owns the five-minute reflection before a real-life decision or transition, not another daily horoscope feed.**

This is a hypothesis for the demo, not a validated market claim. The first move is a **7-Day Clarity Ritual**. The product should invite people who are in the middle of a decision, give them one short reading and one reflection prompt each day, then measure completion, decisions named, return within seven days, and whether the participant reports a clearer next step.
